Learn to Spot Cyber Scams

Understand common online scams and learn how to stay safe.

🎣

Phishing Messages

Fake messages may pretend to come from your bank or a trusted company.

  • Check the sender.
  • Never share your OTP.
  • Avoid suspicious links.
💸

UPI Scams

Scammers may trick you into approving a payment.

  • Verify the receiver.
  • Never share your UPI PIN.
  • Check the amount before paying.
🚨

Digital Arrest Scams

Criminals may impersonate police officers and threaten arrest to demand money.

  • Stay calm.
  • Don't transfer money under pressure.
  • Verify the claim independently.
